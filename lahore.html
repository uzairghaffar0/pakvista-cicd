Lahore – City of Gardens

Welcome to Lahore, the cultural heart of Pakistan. Known as the City of Gardens, Lahore boasts a rich history spanning millennia, marked by stunning Mughal architecture and vibrant bazaars.

Badshahi Mosque

The Badshahi Mosque is a marvel of Mughal architecture, commissioned by Emperor Aurangzeb in 1671. It remained the largest mosque in the world for over 300 years and its iconic red sandstone and white marble domes are a symbol of Lahore's grandeur. Visitors are mesmerized by the intricate fresco work and the vast courtyard that can hold tens of thousands of worshippers.

Lahore Fort

Adjacent to the Badshahi Mosque lies the Lahore Fort, a citadel that contains some of the finest examples of Mughal artistry. Rebuilt in the 17th century by Emperor Akbar, it features the stunning Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors) and the beautiful Alamgiri Gate. It stands as a testament to the empire's power and artistic excellence.

Shalimar Gardens

The Shalimar Gardens were built by Shah Jahan in 1641 and are a masterpiece of nature and architecture combined. Laid out as a Persian paradise garden, the complex features three terraces, beautiful pavilions, and hundreds of fountains, offering a serene escape from the bustling city.
