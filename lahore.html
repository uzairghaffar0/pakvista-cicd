Lahore – The City of Gardens

Lahore is Pakistan's second-largest city and the historic capital of the Punjab province. Famous for its rich history, outstanding Mughal architecture, beautiful gardens, and lively food culture, Lahore is often described by the local phrase: "One who has not seen Lahore has not been born."

Key Historical Landmarks

The city is home to several monumental structures, primarily located in and around the Walled City:

  • Badshahi Mosque: Built in 1673 by Emperor Aurangzeb, it was the largest mosque in the world for over 300 years and represents the pinnacle of Mughal architecture.
  • Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila): A magnificent UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring the famous Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors) and the Picture Wall.
  • Shalimar Gardens: Mughal gardens built by Shah Jahan in 1641, showcasing beautiful water cascades and layout designs.
