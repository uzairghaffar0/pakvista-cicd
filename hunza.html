Hunza Valley – Heaven on Earth

Situated in Gilgit-Baltistan in northern Pakistan, Hunza Valley is surrounded by some of the most striking and colossal mountain peaks in the Karakoram range. It is globally famous for its sapphire-blue lakes, majestic peaks, and the legendary longevity of its residents.

Key Natural and Historical Attractions

Hunza is a dream destination for mountaineers, trekkers, and history lovers:

  • Rakaposhi Viewpoint: Located directly on the Karakoram Highway, offering an incredible, unobstructed view of the 7,788m high Rakaposhi peak.
  • Attabad Lake: A stunning, bright turquoise lake formed in 2010, popular for boating and jet-skiing amidst deep canyon mountains.
  • Baltit and Altit Forts: Ancient structures dating back over 700 years, offering a glimpse into the history of the local Mirs of Hunza.
