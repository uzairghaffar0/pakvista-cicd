Welcome to PakVista

Pakistan is a land of incredible contrasts, offering some of the world's most diverse tourist attractions. Whether you are an adventure seeker looking to scale the highest mountain peaks, a history enthusiast eager to trace ancient civilizations, or a traveler wanting to experience vibrant urban cultures, Pakistan welcomes you. PakVista is your ultimate digital portal to plan and explore the country's top five premier tourist destinations.

Top Rated Destinations

Lahore

Known as the cultural heart of Pakistan, Lahore is rich in historical heritage, containing legendary sites like the Badshahi Mosque, Lahore Fort, and Shalimar Gardens.

Explore Lahore

Hunza Valley

Nestled in the high Karakoram range, Hunza offers stunning views of snow-capped mountains, turquoise lakes, ancient forts, and warm, welcoming local cultures.

Explore Hunza

Karachi

Pakistan's largest metropolis and economic hub, the "City of Lights" features Clifton Beach, colonial architecture, and a lively seafood culture.

Explore Karachi

Swat Valley

Often called the Switzerland of Pakistan, Swat Valley is famous for its lush meadows, pine forests, pristine rivers, and historical Buddhist archaeology.

Explore Swat
