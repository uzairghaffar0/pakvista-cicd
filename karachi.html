Karachi – City of Lights

Explore the vibrant coastal city of Karachi, the economic hub of Pakistan, known for its diverse culture, bustling markets, and beautiful seaside.

Clifton Beach

Clifton Beach, also known as Sea View, is one of the most famous and popular beaches in Karachi. Located on the Arabian Sea, it offers breathtaking views of the ocean, especially during sunset. Visitors can enjoy camel and horse rides along the sandy shores, indulge in local street food, and relax with family and friends. The beach stretches for miles and remains a lively spot throughout the year, attracting both locals and tourists seeking a refreshing escape from the bustling city life.

Mohatta Palace

The Mohatta Palace is a magnificent museum that showcases the rich history and cultural heritage of Sindh. Built in 1927 by Shivratan Chandraratan Mohatta, a wealthy Marwari businessman, the palace is a stunning example of Indo-Saracenic architecture. It features intricate stone carvings, beautiful domes, and spacious pavilions. After serving various government functions, it was converted into a museum in 1999 and now hosts various art and cultural exhibitions, preserving the legacy of the region's artistic traditions.

Pakistan Maritime Museum

The Pakistan Maritime Museum is a naval museum located in Karsaz, Karachi. It spans over 28 acres and offers a fascinating journey through Pakistan's naval history. The museum features various indoor galleries, showcasing historical maritime artifacts, naval armaments, and interactive exhibits. The main attractions include an actual Daphne-class submarine (PNS Hangor), a minesweeper ship, and a Breguet Atlantic aircraft. It is an educational and inspiring destination for history enthusiasts and families alike.
