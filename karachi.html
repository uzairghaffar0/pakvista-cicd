Karachi – The City of Lights

Karachi is Pakistan's largest city, primary seaport, and financial capital. Situated along the Arabian Sea, it is a melting pot of diverse cultures, boasting historic landmarks, extensive sandy beaches, delicious food hubs, and an energetic lifestyle that never sleeps.

Key Sightseeing Spots

Karachi offers a wide mix of maritime, historical, and modern cultural attractions:

  • Clifton Beach: A popular public beach and promenade where visitors enjoy camel rides, horse riding, and Arabian sea breezes.
  • Mohatta Palace: A beautiful pink-stone palace built in 1927 by a Hindu businessman, now serving as a prominent museum of art and history.
  • Pakistan Maritime Museum: An expansive naval museum featuring a real submarine, aircraft, and exhibits detailing the country's maritime history.
